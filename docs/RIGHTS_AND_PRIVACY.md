# Rights, privacy and demo disclosures

- All track identities in this public showcase are **fictional**. They do not pretend to be actual artists or label recordings.
- All demo audio is **original, procedurally synthesized** by `assets/audio.js` from simple note arrays stored alongside the fictional tracks. No copyrighted song samples, music videos, album artwork, song lyrics or recordings are bundled or streamed.
- The logo and decorative album art are vector/CSS assets created for this showcase.
- This package does not interact with Deezer, Spotify, YouTube, LRCLIB, MusicBrainz or other external music providers. The original local AFTERTONE prototype is a separate artifact with different rights and usage requirements.
- There are no signups, accounts, analytics SDKs, cookies, external fonts, advertising networks or service-worker caches.
- The demo saves favorites, choices and tournament podiums in `localStorage` under `aftertone:portfolio-demo:v1`. Nothing is uploaded. Use the **Reset demo** button to remove its saved data.
- Before publishing the **full local music-game implementation** rather than this safe showcase, obtain appropriate permission for preview playback, game mechanics and lyrics display. These rights are not granted by this project's source-code license.

The site provides a technical showcase, not legal advice or a guarantee of no third-party claims.
