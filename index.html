03 / THE PRODUCT JOURNEY

Built by listening to feedback.

The strongest changes came from discovering where the original prototype didn't feel good enough—and rethinking the experience instead of adding another setting.

01
THE FIRST SPARK

Swipe, keep, explore.

Started with anonymous short previews, simple decisions and a taste map. Learned quickly that limited catalogs and mystery metadata made discovery frustrating.

02
THE REAL CHALLENGE

Separate discovery from playback.

Explored multiple providers, then designed clearer boundaries between canonical song information, playback availability, catalog permissions and what a local prototype can do.

03
WHAT MADE IT A GAME

Tournaments, not thumbs.

Introduced Music Duels, tournament themes, saved podiums and champion brackets to give each session a destination and a sense of progression.

04
WHAT'S NEXT

Polish, validate, present.

Wrapped the work in a self-contained, rights-safe interactive showcase. The full local application stays separate until provider and media-display permissions are addressed.
